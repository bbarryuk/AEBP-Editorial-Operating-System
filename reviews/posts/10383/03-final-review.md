## 10383-F07

> above a pre-emergency baseline

The video implies the baseline is always a pre-emergency amount. Penal Code §396(j)(11) includes a HUD-based baseline for units without recent rental activity. The article body correctly uses statutory baseline.

**Replacement**

During a covered emergency, the law generally bars rent more than ten percent above the baseline set by law, which depends on the property’s rental history. Limited defenses apply.
