## Gated categories

### GATE-LEGAL-ACCURACY — FAIL
- **10515-F02 (A)** Quote: "the repair clock starts when you get notice". Root cause: Civil Code 1942(a) is specific to notice of untenantable conditions; phrasing sweeps in all reports. **Fix:** Replace 'the repair clock starts when you get notice' with 'Once you receive notice of a condition affecting habitability, respond within a reasonable time for the circumstances; document and investigate every leak report.'
- **10515-F03 (A)** Quote: "Emergency entry without notice (§1954)". Root cause: Operational emergency classification is not by itself a legal determination that emergency entry is warranted. **Fix:** Replace 'Emergency entry without notice (§1954)' with 'Emergency entry without advance notice only where circumstances constitute an actual emergency under §1954; otherwise use statutory notice or applicable consent.'
- **10515-F06 (A)** Quote: "Two kinds of calls should always move up a tier: anything involving electrical, and anything where the tenant mentions a musty smell or spots on the wall.". Root cause: Musty odor and stains call for prompt investigation but aren't necessarily legally defined emergencies. **Fix:** Replace with 'Treat water near electrical fixtures as a potential immediate safety hazard. Prioritize prompt inspection of musty odors or suspected mold, and escalate based on the actual risk.'
