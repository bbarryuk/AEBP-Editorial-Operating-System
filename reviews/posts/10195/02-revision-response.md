> Before: Tenants can always call the police | A lease term that limits a tenant's right to call law enforcement or emergency help is void.

> After: Protected calls for police or emergency help | Civil Code §1946.8 protects calls made by or on behalf of someone facing abuse, a crime or an emergency who believes help is needed.
