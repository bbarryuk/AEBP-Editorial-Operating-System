# Berkeley Rent Board Registration — Fees, Deadlines, New-Tenancy Window

Existed for one reason: a ChatGPT pre-publish audit of the September Week 1 cornerstone (post 9008, "East Bay Rent Control Comparison") caught that a draft comparison table described Berkeley's registration as "ongoing, no single fixed date" — true for updating existing tenancy information, but wrong about the annual registration fee, which has a real, penalized deadline. The repo had excellent Berkeley just-cause documentation (`berkeley-just-cause.md`) but no equivalent source of truth for registration mechanics, so this had to be rediscovered during review instead of being on file. This entry exists so that doesn't happen again.

## What's actually true

- **Annual registration fees have a fixed due date, not an open-ended cycle.** FY 2026–27 fees were due **July 2, 2026**. Berkeley's ordinance imposes a **100% penalty** on payments not received or postmarked by the due date (a waiver of part or all of the penalty was available for owners who paid in full by a stated make-good date in a prior cycle — check the current year's notice for whether a waiver window exists that year, don't assume one is standing policy).
- **New tenancies have their own, separate 15-day window.** Owners must update tenancy information with the Rent Board within **15 days of the start of a new tenancy** — this is a rolling, per-tenancy requirement, distinct from the annual fee deadline above. For tenancies beginning on or after December 20, 2024, landlords must also give tenants written notice within the same 15-day window.
- **Status-change registration is a third, separate clock.** If a unit's exemption status changes from exempt to non-exempt, registration must occur within **60 days of the change**.
- So "registration" in Berkeley is really three different deadlines depending on what triggered it (annual fee, new tenancy, status change) — collapsing this to "ongoing, no fixed date" understates the compliance risk of missing the annual fee deadline specifically.
