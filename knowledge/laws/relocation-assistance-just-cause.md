# Relocation Assistance for No-Fault Just-Cause Terminations

Existed for one reason: a Week 4 Monday cornerstone draft (post 8883, "Ending a Month-to-Month Tenancy in California") stated only AB 1482's flat one-month-rent relocation figure and didn't account for Oakland's, Berkeley's, or Emeryville's own — much larger — local relocation ordinances. Brian caught the gap in review (2026-08-19): Emeryville always requires relocation assistance for a no-fault termination (not just "sometimes," the way AB 1482's statewide figure might read), and Oakland and Berkeley each set their own amounts under their own ordinances rather than defaulting to AB 1482's number. This file exists so that distinction doesn't have to be re-derived next time a post touches no-fault terminations or relocation assistance.

## The core point: AB 1482's one-month figure is a statewide floor, not what most AEBP-service-area cities actually require

Where no local ordinance applies, AB 1482 (Civil Code §1946.2) requires relocation assistance equal to one month's rent, or an equivalent rent waiver, for a no-fault just-cause termination. But **Oakland, Berkeley, and Emeryville each have their own relocation ordinances that supersede this figure for covered units** — and all three are substantially larger than one month's rent in most cases. A post or answer that states "$one month's rent" as the relocation figure for an Oakland, Berkeley, or Emeryville property is very likely wrong.

## Oakland — Uniform Residential Tenant Relocation Ordinance

**Confidence: Level 2 (required by local ordinance), sourced from a secondary legal-practice summary (Bornstein Law, 2026) corroborating the City's own June 2025 info sheet; not independently re-confirmed against a live oaklandca.gov fetch this session (the direct PDF fetch failed — see Currency note below).**

For the period **August 1, 2025 – July 31, 2026** (the ordinance adjusts annually on August 1, the same cycle as Oakland's RAP rent cap — see `knowledge/laws/rent-caps.md`):

| Unit size | Base relocation payment |
|---|---|
| Studio / 1BR | $8,106.68 |
| 2BR | $9,977.45 |
| 3+ BR | $12,315.92 |

Plus an **additional $2,500 per unit** if the household includes a lower-income tenant, someone 60 or older, a disabled person, or minor children.

Payment is split: the first half is due within 15 calendar days of serving the termination notice, the second half within 15 calendar days of the tenant vacating.

**Currency flag:** this file's `last_verified` date (2026-08-19) is *after* the cited cycle's own end date (July 31, 2026) — meaning an updated August 2026–July 2027 figure should exist but was not found via web search this session (no city page or legal-practice summary surfaced a confirmed new figure as of this check). **Do not cite these exact dollar amounts in new content without re-verifying against a live `oaklandca.gov` source first** — treat this as `EVD-BLOCK-PENDING-VERIFICATION` for the specific dollar figures (the *existence* of Oakland's much-larger-than-AB-1482 relocation ordinance is well-corroborated and safe to state; the *current* dollar amounts are not, until re-checked).

## Berkeley — Relocation Assistance for No-Fault (Owner Move-In / Ellis Act) Terminations

**Confidence: Level 1 (required by local ordinance, City-published) — confirmed 2026-08-19.** Source: [Berkeley Rent Board, "2026 Adjustments of Relocation Assistance Payments"](https://rentboard.berkeleyca.gov/elected-rent-board/news/2026-adjustments-relocation-assistance-payments).

Effective **January 1, 2026** (a 1.5% CPI-based adjustment from the prior amount):

- **Standard relocation payment: $19,413**
- **Additional payment for qualifying households (e.g., low-income, senior, disabled, or with minor children — confirm exact qualifying criteria against the Rent Board page before citing specifics): $6,471**

This is Berkeley's **eviction relocation** ordinance (owner move-in, Ellis Act, and similar no-fault grounds) — distinct from Berkeley's separate temporary-displacement/repair relocation program (`berkeleyca.gov/doing-business/operating-berkeley/landlords/tenant-relocation-repairs`), which covers code-required repair displacement, not eviction. Don't conflate the two when citing a figure.

Berkeley's cycle is calendar-year (Jan 1 effective date), unlike Oakland's and AB 1482's Aug 1 cycle — re-verify around Q4 2026 for a possible 2027 adjustment, consistent with how `knowledge/laws/rent-caps.md` already tracks Berkeley's AGA on a separate calendar-year cycle from Oakland/AB 1482.

## Emeryville — Residential Landlord and Tenant Relations Ordinance (Just Cause Ordinance)

**Confidence: Level 2 (required by local ordinance) — sourced from an AI-summarized fetch of a hosted copy of the ordinance text (echofairhousing.org), not independently re-confirmed against Emeryville's own municipal code site this session. Treat as corroborated-but-not-primary-verified; re-check against Emeryville's own site before treating dollar-formula details as fully closed.**

Two points, both distinct from Oakland's and Berkeley's ordinances:

1. **No waiting period.** Emeryville's Just Cause Ordinance applies from day one of the tenancy — same day-one pattern as Oakland and Berkeley, and unlike AB 1482's 12-/24-month statewide threshold. A no-cause termination under Civil Code §1946.1 is effectively never available in Emeryville, the same way it isn't in Oakland or Berkeley, because just cause already applies from the start.
2. **Relocation assistance is required for every no-fault termination** (Emeryville Municipal Code §5-40.04(a)), except where the no-fault termination was caused by a natural disaster or similar phenomenon beyond the landlord's control. The amount is formula-based, not a flat dollar figure, and scales with landlord size:
   - **Small landlords (4 or fewer units):** the greater of (a) one month of the current HUD Fair Market Rent for the Oakland-Fremont HUD Metro FMR Area, or (b) one month of the tenant's actual rent.
   - **Large landlords (more than 4 units):** the greater of (a) five times the current HUD Fair Market Rent for the Oakland-Fremont HUD Metro FMR Area, or (b) four times the tenant's actual monthly rent.

   Payment is due by the last day of the tenancy for which the landlord received rent.

Brian's shorthand ("Emeryville always requires 1 month for no-cause") reflects the small-landlord tier, which likely covers most of AEBP's Emeryville portfolio — but the ordinance itself sets a materially larger figure for any landlord with more than 4 units, and content should represent both tiers rather than flattening to "one month" universally.

## Practical implication for termination/relocation content

Before stating a relocation-assistance dollar figure or rule in any post, check three things in order: (1) does a local ordinance apply at all (Oakland, Berkeley, Emeryville — each overrides AB 1482's figure, they don't stack with it); (2) if Oakland or Emeryville, does landlord/unit size change the applicable tier; (3) is the cited dollar figure still within its current cycle (Oakland: Aug 1 cycle; Berkeley: Jan 1 cycle) or does it need re-verification. Where no local ordinance applies, AB 1482's flat one-month (or equivalent waiver) figure is the correct statewide answer.

## What this file does not cover

Relocation assistance for temporary displacement due to code-required repairs (a different concept from eviction relocation) is out of scope here — see Berkeley's separate repair-relocation program for that city; Oakland and Emeryville may have analogous distinctions not yet researched for this file. The at-fault just-cause grounds themselves (nonpayment, lease violation, etc.) are covered in `knowledge/laws/lease-expiration-and-renewal.md` and `knowledge/laws/eviction-notice-timing.md`, not restated here.
