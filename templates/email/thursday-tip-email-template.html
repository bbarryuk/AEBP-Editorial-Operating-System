2026 Landlord Tip

A tenant who doesn't know how to report a broken lobby lock can't tell you about it. Under California's lock law, the time to fix a broken required lock starts when you have notice of it. A one-page safety sheet makes reporting easy, and it gives you a written record that you told tenants how to reach you.

At AEBP, every new tenant gets a welcome sheet: routine repairs go through the portal, urgent problems go to our 24/7 line, and a broken entry lock gets a vendor the same day. Our October newsletter added the rest: report lights and doors, don't prop secured doors, and the Oakland, Berkeley and Emeryville non-emergency police numbers.

⚠️ Leave "secure building," "safe neighborhood" and any promise about outcomes off the sheet. Describe the features that actually exist.
💡
This Week's Thursday Tip

Describe the building and how to reach you. Skip the adjectives.

Include your maintenance line and portal, 911 vs. your city's non-emergency line (Oakland 510-777-3333, Berkeley 510-981-5900, Emeryville 510-596-3700), door and package rules, and who to contact for a lock change. Email it to current tenants this week too.
